🦈 JAWS Tracking Spike

Grey box, no art. Three numbers decide the project: tracking FPS > 24, poses steady (no flicker to 0), and jitter < 2.5 px while holding still. Smoothing on = the number that matters for the real shark. Grant camera access when asked; serve over localhost or https.

A recorded clip runs through the same tracker, looping, with the skeleton drawn over it. Fine for comparing angles, misfires and jitter; tracking fps is capped by the clip's own frame rate.
